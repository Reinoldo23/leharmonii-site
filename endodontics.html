Learn more about root canal treatment

Root canal treatment, also called endodontic treatment, is the procedure indicated when the dental pulp, the inner tissue containing nerves and blood vessels, is affected by deep decay, trauma or infection.

Without treatment, the infection spreads, can cause an abscess and bone destruction, and may lead to tooth loss. Root canal treatment eliminates the infection, relieves pain and saves the natural tooth.

Dr. Morgane Marion sees patients in Florianópolis and the surrounding area, including São José and Palhoça. Besides root canal treatment, she is a specialist in endodontic retreatment, indicated when a tooth that already had a root canal shows pain, swelling or infection again.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is endodontic treatment recommended?

In cases of intense or persistent toothache, prolonged sensitivity to cold or heat, pain when chewing or touching the tooth, gum swelling or a fistula, and tooth darkening after trauma.

Does root canal treatment hurt?

The procedure is done under anesthesia, and Dr. Morgane Marion's protocol prioritizes patient comfort and well-being, welcoming those who feel fear or anxiety.

Can a root canal be done in a single session?

Yes, when indicated. After the evaluation, the endodontist decides whether treatment can be completed in a single session.

What is endodontic treatment?

It is root canal treatment: it removes the damaged pulp tissue, cleans and disinfects the root canals, then fills and seals them, eliminating infection and preserving the natural tooth.
